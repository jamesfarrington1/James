welcome

In this interactive experience, you will explore the concept of mortality as it relates to the web. Navigate through different states of being and discover how life and death intertwine in the digital realm.

*These pages aren't meant to function as a traditional website, but rather as an aesthetic journey through the life cycle of web technologies and platforms. Certain buttons or links will not have any effect when clicked.

your journey

Use the "Time Travel" ability to transition between states of being. Each state will show the degradation of web space as we go back in time.
